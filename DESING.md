Design System & Visual Identity Document: Chifa Fung Shing

1. Executive Summary & Brand Identity

Chifa Fung Shing is a traditional Peruvian-Chinese (Chifa) restaurant located in La Molina, Lima. Its identity combines authentic Chinese heritage, symbolized by imperial dragon motifs, with warm local hospitality.

Brand Positioning: Premium yet welcoming Chifa dining with traditional recipes, generous family portions, and rich cultural aesthetic.

Hero Branding Motif: The Chinese Dragon (representing prosperity, strength, and culinary mastery).

Tone of Voice: Traditional, celebratory, appetizing, and hospitable.

2. Color Palette (Chifa Traditional & Dragon Aesthetic)

Primary Colors

Imperial Red / Crimson: #B71C1C

Usage: Primary CTA buttons, key banners, main headers, dragon accent details, navigation highlights.

Rich Charcoal / Obsidian Black: #121212 / #1A1A1A

Usage: Primary text, dark backgrounds, high-contrast hero sections, footer background.

Secondary & Accent Colors

Imperial Gold / Warm Amber: #D4AF37 / #E6B800

Usage: Secondary buttons, dragon icons, subtle borders, rating stars, highlighting special dishes.

Cream / Warm Silk White: #FDFBF7 / #F7F3EA

Usage: Page backgrounds, card containers, readable text panels.

Neutral Colors

Earthy Brown / Roasted Spice: #3E2723

Usage: Subtitles, divider lines, secondary badges.

Muted Grey: #666666

Usage: Body text copy on light panels, subtle metadata.

3. Typography

Primary Display / Headlines: Elegant Serif or Asian-inspired Heading font (e.g., Cinzel, Playfair Display, or Noto Serif).

Body & UI Text: Clean Modern Sans-Serif (e.g., Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, or Montserrat).

## 4. UI Components & Patterns

### 4.1 Header & Navigation
* **Top Utility Banner (Optional):** Contextual promotional banner (e.g., seasonal specials, gift cards).
* **Main Navigation Bar:**
  * Sticky header with blur or high opacity backplate.
  * Left/Center: Logo ("dim t").
  * Links: Menus, Restaurants, Offers, About, Careers, Contact.
  * Action Button: High-visibility CTA ("Book a Table").

### 4.2 Hero Section
* **Layout:** Full-width or split layout showcasing vibrant food photography (Dim Sum baskets, Asian dishes).
* **Elements:** Headline, subheadline, primary CTA ("Book Now" / "Explore Menu").

### 4.3 Buttons & Interactive Elements
* **Primary CTA:**
  * Background: Warm Terracotta / Coral Red.
  * Text: White, Semi-bold.
  * Border Radius: Slight rounded corners (`4px` or `8px`) or fully rounded pill style (`9999px`).
  * Hover state: Slight darkening or elevation shadow.
* **Secondary CTA:**
  * Outline / Ghost style or dark neutral fill with white text.
* **Form Inputs:**
  * Clean, minimal borders (`#CCCCCC`), focus state with accent outline.

### 4.4 Content Cards
* **Menu Dish Cards:**
  * Top image with subtle hover zoom effect.
  * Title, short description, price, and dietary tags (GF, VG, V).
* **Location Cards:**
  * Restaurant name (e.g., Hampstead, Winchester, Whiteley).
  * Quick links for "Book Table", "View Menu", and "Directions".

### 4.5 Promotional & Newsletter Section
* **Birthday / Offer Signup:** Form box integrated into a visually distinct background block (dark or textured).
* **Fields:** First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Email, Preferred Location dropdown.

### 4.6 Footer
* **Layout:** Multi-column layout.
  * Column 1: Navigation quick links (Eat, Book, Takeaway).
  * Column 2: Secondary pages (Offers, Gift Cards, Careers).
  * Column 3: Legal & Help (Allergens, Terms, Cookie Notice).
  * Bottom Bar: Copyright notice, social icons, accessibility statement.

## 5. Imagery & Visual Assets

* **Photography Style:** High-contrast, rich saturation food photography focusing on warmth, steam, fresh ingredients, and dim sum baskets.
* **Iconography:** Minimalist line icons for dietary preferences (leaf for vegetarian/vegan, wheat for gluten-free) and navigation items.
* **Textures & Patterns:** Subtle bamboo/steam motifs or organic paper textures on background sections.

---

## 6. Layout & Responsiveness Grid

* **Grid System:** 12-column grid for desktop (`max-width: 1200px` or `1280px`).
* **Mobile Breakpoints:**
  * Mobile: `< 640px` (Single column layout, collapsible hamburger menu).
  * Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (2-column grids for cards and menus).
  * Desktop: `> 1024px` (3 or 4-column grids).
* **Spacing Scale:** Standard 8px spatial grid system (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`).
